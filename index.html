The weekly cycle

One post, one week, one payout

Every coin runs the same loop. A new home post every Monday keeps the contest fresh and puts the coin back in people's feeds.

MON

Creator posts

The creator posts the week's home post from ReplyPay. It's linked to the coin automatically.

MON → THU

People reply

Anyone replies or quote posts. The leaderboard ranks them live by likes from real accounts.

FRI 00:00

Finalists locked

The top 20 replies become finalists. Holdings are snapshotted so votes can't be bought late.

#1#2#3#4…
FRI → SUN

Holders vote

Coin holders vote for their favourite replies. Vote weight grows with holdings, capped per wallet.

SUN 20:00

Winners get paid

The pool is swapped to USD and split by vote share. Each winner gets dollars through X Money.

Fair play

Hard to farm, easy to check

Real accounts only

Only likes from accounts 90+ days old count toward finalists. Creators can require verified accounts.

Snapshot before voting

Holdings are frozen Friday 00:00 UTC. Buying in during the vote doesn't add weight.

Whale cap

Vote weight scales with holdings but no wallet can hold more than 5% of the vote.

One win per person

One payout per X account per contest, no matter how many replies they post. The creator can't win their own pool.

Public record

Every contest shows the home post, finalists, vote totals, winners, amounts and receipts. Anyone can check.

No wallet to get paid

Winners don't need a Solana wallet or a pump.fun account. Dollars land in X Money.

What if a week gets no replies?

The reply pool rolls over to next week's contest, so the next pool is bigger.

What if the creator skips posting?

If there's no new home post by Tuesday, last week's post stays active and replies to it keep counting.

Do I need to hold the coin to win?

No. Anyone on X can reply and win. You only need to hold the coin to vote.

Can I change the pool split later?

No. It's locked at launch so repliers and holders know what they're playing for.

What is ReplyPay not?

It's not an exchange or a wallet, and it doesn't hold your coin supply. It launches on pump.fun, runs the weekly reply contest and pays out through X Money.

Why are big wins spread over days?

X Money limits each account to $750 in a rolling 24 hours, so larger wins are sent in parts. Every part shows up in the receipt.
