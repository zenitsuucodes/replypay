Fair play

Hard to farm, easy to check

One entry per account

Only one entry counts per X account each day, however many links they paste. The creator can't win their own pool.

Must answer the home post

An entry has to be a reply to or a quote of the coin's home post (the one with its rp- code). Anything else is rejected.

Deleted posts are dropped

If an entry is deleted before 00:00 UTC, it's removed from that day's ranking.

50% cap per winner

No single winner can take more than half the pool. The rest is re-shared by score.

Public record

Every contest shows the home post, each entry, its score, the winners, amounts and receipts. Anyone can check.

No wallet to get paid

Winners don't need a Solana wallet or a pump.fun account. Dollars land in X Money.

How are winners picked?

Automatically, by engagement. Each entry scores likes + 2×reposts + 3×quotes + replies + views/100. At 00:00 UTC each day, the top 3, 5 or 10 (set at launch) split that day's reply pool in proportion to score, and no one gets more than 50% of it.

Do I need to sign in to enter?

No. Reply to or quote the home post on X, then paste your post link on the coin page. The payout goes to whoever wrote the post.

How do you know the creator is real?

Every home post includes the coin's code (like rp-7K2Q). Posting it from the account proves they own it.

What if a day gets no entries?

That day's pool rolls over to tomorrow's contest, so the next pool is bigger.

Does the creator need to post every day?

No. The home post carries over day to day, and replies or quotes on it count for the day they're entered. The creator can post a fresh one any day for more reach.

Can I change the pool split later?

No. It's locked at launch so everyone knows what they're playing for.

What is ReplyPay not?

It's not an exchange or a wallet, and it doesn't hold your coin supply. It launches on pump.fun, runs the daily reply contest and pays out through X Money.

How are winners paid?

Daily at 00:00 UTC, in full, in dollars, straight to their X Money. Every payment gets a public receipt.
